## 1. Two dead links, both on your flagship research section

**Severity: high. Fix before anything else on this page.**

Lines 17 and 19 of `README.md` point at:

```
https://github.com/yogeshhk/BharatVidya/tree/master/src/ask_yogasutra
https://github.com/yogeshhk/BharatVidya/tree/master/src/ask_vichar-chitre
```

Both return **HTTP 404**. Verified two ways: GitHub returns 404 for both URLs, and neither path
exists in the local `BharatVidya` working copy. There is no `src/` directory in that repo at all
(the only code lives under `Samskrit/src/` and `Code/claudecode/`), and a recursive search for
`ask_yogasutra` or anything matching `vichar` finds nothing.

This matters more than a typical broken link. These are two of the three bullets under **Research
and Collaboration** -- the section a prospective client, collaborator or student is most likely to
click. Two of the three currently 404.

Options, in order of preference:

1. If the work exists elsewhere (another repo, private, or a local folder not yet pushed), point
   the links there.
2. If it exists but was never published, describe the work without a link, or say "write-up in
   progress".
3. If it was never built, remove those two bullets. An honest two-item list beats a three-item list
   where two items 404.

**A note on timing.** With `BharatVidya` being archived, option 1 is not going to be satisfied by
anything inside that repo. Decide what these two entries should actually say.

## 5. Formatting and appeal

**Severity: low. Take or leave.**

- **No tagline.** The page opens with "Hi, I'm Yogesh" then goes straight into a service list. One
  line stating what you do -- AI advisory, training, geometric deep learning research -- would
  orient a first-time visitor before they start parsing bullets. This is the single highest-value
  cosmetic change.
- **The header GIF is hosted in someone else's repo.** Line 1 pulls from
  `raw.githubusercontent.com/ABSphreak/ABSphreak/...`. It currently returns 200, but you do not
  control that repo; if it is renamed or cleaned up, your profile header silently breaks. Copy the
  asset into this repo and reference it locally.
- **Section balance.** "Social Contributions (Pro Bono)" is 11 bullets, longer than AI Advisory (3)
  and Research (3) combined, so the commercial offering is visually outweighed by the pro bono work.
  If the page is meant to generate advisory business, consider rebalancing, or splitting Social
  Contributions into "Speaking and Writing" and "Community".
- **Dated links.** The Open Office Hours bullet points at two LinkedIn activity posts from 2022 and
  2023. Worth confirming the Saturday 1:30-3:30 PM IST slot is still accurate; a stale standing
  commitment is worse than none.
- **No profile stats or visual anchor.** GitHub profile READMEs commonly carry a stats card, a
  language breakdown, or a pinned-repo strip. Optional, but the page is currently all text after
  the header GIF.
